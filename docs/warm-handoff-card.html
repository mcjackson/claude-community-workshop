How to run a handoff
  1. Safety first. Immediate danger or dangerous weather with no shelter? Call 911/crisis and transport now, don't issue a card.
  2. Ask what they need, in their words. Get consent to help.
  3. Capture the minimum: first name + initial, need, eligibility lane, accommodations. Not their history.
  4. Pick a place the person is eligible for, then confirm by phone (room, eligibility, form).
  5. Arrange the ride and call ahead with the first name + code.
  6. Hand off the card, or (no printer) say/write: where to go, the code, who's expecting them.
