Warm Handoff Summary — Pilot Flow

Challenge 4 · one bounded decision point: "what safe next step, confirmed by whom?"

1

Responder opens the tool

ER discharge nurse, shelter intake worker, street outreach, or 211 operator. The person experiencing homelessness is the subject, not the operator.

2

Enters the situation

A few fields only: immediate needs, safety constraints, key eligibility factors (e.g. family, DV, youth, veteran, medical). No SSN, no sensitive detail stored.

3

Tool matches the service directory

source + date

Curated, clearly-synthetic Birmingham directory. Every option shows eligibility notes and a "last verified" date. It never claims a live open bed.

4

Generates the Warm Handoff Summary

Three sections: current situation · confirmed-safe next steps with a named follow-up owner · a "must confirm before handoff" checklist.

5

Must-confirm checklist

uncertainty flagged

Anything the tool can't guarantee (bed availability, eligibility) is flagged red, not asserted as fact.

6

Human confirms with receiving agency

human in the loop

Responder calls to confirm the bed and eligibility. This is the required human-review point — the tool assists, a trained person decides.

7

Summary shared + follow-up owned

accountability

Receiving agency gets the summary (print or secure link) so the person doesn't re-tell their story. Named owner tracks until the person is received.
