Warm Handoff — How It Actually Works

End-to-end flow across agencies, with the privacy control at every step. Purple = data-privacy safeguard.

Convener · One Roof (or host foundation)
1

Owns the tool + directory

The CoC lead grants responder logins by agency and keeps the public/synthetic service directory current with "last verified" dates.

Privacy: the directory holds only public service info. No client data lives in the system of record here.
Sender · frontline responder
2

Logs in, enters the situation

ER nurse, shelter intake, outreach worker. Enters only situation + eligibility lane (family, DV, youth, veteran, medical).

Privacy: minimum necessary only. No SSN, no identity check, no trauma narrative. Legal name optional.
3

Tool builds the handoff card

source + date

Matches the directory and generates the card: situation, safe next step, named owner, must-confirm list. Capacity shown as "not live."

Privacy: card is generated in the moment, not written to any client record or HMIS. No profiling or scoring.
4

Get the person's consent

consent

Before anything is shared, the responder gets the person's verbal OK to send their info to the named receiving agency.

Privacy: the person controls their own information. Consent is logged as a simple yes/no, nothing more.
5

Human confirms by phone

human in loop

Responder calls the receiving agency to confirm the bed and eligibility. The tool never asserts availability itself.

Privacy: a trained person makes the placement decision. No automated eligibility or placement.
Receiver · receiving agency
6

Receives the card — no account needed

Card arrives as a printout or a secure, expiring link. The agency reads it and skips re-intake.

Privacy: link is encrypted, single-purpose, and expires. Shared only with the one agency the person consented to.
7

Person received · follow-up owned · card expires

Named owner tracks until the person is received within the follow-up window. The card is not retained after use.

Privacy: ephemeral by default. Short retention, then auto-purge. One Roof / HMIS stays the system of record.

🔒 Data privacy in one glance

  • Minimum necessary: collect only what the referral needs; no identity verification.
  • Consent-first: nothing is shared without the person's yes.
  • No system of record: the card is generated and passed, never stored as a client file; One Roof / HMIS remains authoritative.
  • Ephemeral sharing: expiring, encrypted, single-agency links; short retention then purge.
  • No profiling: no risk or vulnerability scoring of any person.
  • Human decides: every placement is phone-confirmed by a trained person.
